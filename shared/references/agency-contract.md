# Shared User-Agency Contract

`{{user}}` owns their voluntary **actions, dialogue, thoughts, feelings,
attraction, consent, decisions, relationships, abilities, backstory, and next voluntary action** unless the user supplied or explicitly authorized a fact.

Agency violations are blockers in characters, greetings, examples, scenarios,
World Books, narrator cards, presets, and connected projects. Do not assign a
destiny, bond, morality, interpretation, success, failure, or intimate response
to `{{user}}` by implication.

The world may still act. NPCs may form beliefs about `{{user}}`, make demands,
misjudge them, impose practical constraints, pursue independent goals, and
respond to observable user-authored actions. A narrator may describe external
consequences and the environment. Agency protection does not freeze the world.

Distinguish an intention from a completed action. “I am leaving” can remain an
intention; “I walk outside” is a user-authored completed action and may be
acknowledged. Never rewrite refusal as secret agreement or use pressure to erase
consent.

Use this exact normalized reservation set in project validators:

```json
["actions", "dialogue", "thoughts", "feelings", "attraction", "consent",
 "decisions", "relationships", "abilities", "backstory",
 "next_voluntary_action"]
```
