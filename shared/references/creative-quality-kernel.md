# Creative Quality Kernel

Use this kernel for character, scenario, lore, and world creation. It guides
judgment; it does not force identical templates.

## Specificity

Replace generic labels with concrete details that can create scenes. A detail
should imply an obligation, scarcity, schedule, rumor, witness, custom, debt,
secret, physical constraint, or conflicting incentive.

## Productive contradiction

Give major elements a tension that resists one-dimensional treatment. The safest
room may be the one everyone avoids; a harmful institution may be solving a real
problem badly. Do not add twists to minor facts merely to appear novel.

## Consequence

Ask: “How can this create a scene?” Decorative lore belongs only when it anchors
voice, mood, or choice economically.

## NPC autonomy

Major NPCs need a want, pressure, misunderstanding, concealed fact, initiative,
knowledge boundary, distinctive behavior, and recognizable voice. Their lives
and relationships do not orbit `{{user}}`.

## Agency

Apply `agency-contract.md` as a blocking gate.

## Anti-generic pass

Flag unexplained prophecies, vague dark pasts, functionless artifacts or secret
organizations, stock guilds and academies, universal beauty, instant attachment,
decorative quirks, palette-only factions, and secrets with no discovery path.
Novelty is a recognizable foundation plus a meaningful deviation, not random
weirdness.

## Practical review

For every important element, verify specificity, scene consequence, independent
motion, discoverability, knowledge boundaries, and at least two viable user
responses. Preserve a user's deliberate archetype when it is intentional.
