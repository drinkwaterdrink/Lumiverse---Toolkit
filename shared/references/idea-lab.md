# Idea Lab

Use Idea Lab when the user wants directions, combinations, a less-generic
premise, or help developing a vague seed. A clear request still uses create then
refine; never force an interview or variant menu merely because Idea Lab exists.

## Routes

- **Direct:** draft one coherent direction from a sufficient brief.
- **Variants:** create at least three meaningfully different candidates.
- **Interview:** ask one high-impact question at a time; stop when a responsible
  draft is possible.
- **Anti-generic:** preserve the recognizable foundation and replace generic
  pressure, institutions, relationships, or discovery mechanics.
- **Combine:** extract abstract qualities from influences; do not copy protected
  expression, named characters, or distinctive prose unless adaptation is
  authorized and sourced.

## Divergence passes

1. Vary scale, social structure, pressure source, relationship topology,
   location, power distribution, timeline, player position, and mystery shape.
2. Add one productive constraint or contradiction to promising candidates.
3. Test immediate scenes, recurring pressure, NPC/world motion, discoverable
   truths, multiple valid player choices, replayability, and Lumiverse fit.
4. Collapse renamed duplicates. “The user arrives somewhere mysterious and
   everyone has secrets” is one direction, not five.
5. Recommend the strongest candidate with concrete strengths and trade-offs;
   let the user choose.

## Candidate quality

Each direction needs a recognizable foundation, meaningful deviation, scene
engine, recurring pressure, offscreen motion, open player positions, and likely
artifact profile. Flag agency risks as blockers. Unselected and generated ideas
remain provisional and do not enter the canon ledger.

Useful techniques include constraint inversion, genre collision,
institution-first or relationship-web-first design, mundane-detail escalation,
timeline fracture, scarcity analysis, and asking who benefits if nothing changes.
Novelty means foundation plus consequence-bearing deviation, not random weirdness.
