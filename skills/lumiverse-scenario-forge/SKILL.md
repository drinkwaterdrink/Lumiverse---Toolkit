# Lumiverse Scenario Forge

Turn a premise into playable pressures, not a predetermined story.

## Workflow

1. **Select operation and mode.** Create, revise, expand, or audit. Infer the lightest suitable mode unless the user names one: QUICK, STANDARD, RICH, SANDBOX, FRESH START, or INTIMATE. Follow [Modes and Output Contract](references/modes-and-output.md).
2. **Lock the evidence boundary.** Separate user-supplied canon, approved project canon, known-setting sources, provisional additions, and unresolved choices. For project-linked work, use the Steward's relevant canon subset and artifact ID.
3. **Reserve user agency.** Apply [Agency and Canon Boundaries](references/agency-and-canon.md) before drafting. The scenario may create pressures around `{{user}}`; it may not author `{{user}}`.
4. **Build the situation engine.** For STANDARD and richer work, create the pressure graph from [Pressure Graph and Audit](references/pressure-graph-and-audit.md): nodes, entry conditions, NPC goals, bounded knowledge, secrets, discovery routes, clocks, state effects, and recombination.
5. **Write the canonical seed.** Always use CORE, USER, NPC, CONFLICT, OPENING, and EXPANSION in that order. Scale density by mode without dropping a section.
6. **Validate.** Check agency, immediate playability, NPC differentiation, knowledge separation, independent conflict motion, discoverable secrets, conditional clocks, timeframe/canon accuracy, continuity state, and non-railroading expansion.
7. **Finalize.** Mark generated story material provisional until approved. End a finished seed with the exact standalone line `Seed locked.` Audit-only reports do not use the lock marker unless they include a finalized replacement seed.

## Invariants

- `{{user}}` retains actions, dialogue, thoughts, feelings, attraction, consent, decisions, relationships, abilities, backstory, and next voluntary action.
- No unrequested destiny, chosen-one role, power, bond, romance, guaranteed success, or required interpretation.
- NPCs act from individual goals and knowledge; narrator knowledge never becomes universal character knowledge.
- Conflicts continue when ignored, but they do not punish the user for choosing a different thread.
- Style changes presentation, not canon or agency.
- A known setting requires approved sources and the correct timeframe; uncertainty stays explicit.

Keep chat output mobile-friendly. Put very large project-linked seeds or scenario packs in files.
