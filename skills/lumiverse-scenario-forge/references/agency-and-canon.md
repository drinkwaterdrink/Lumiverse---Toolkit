# Agency and Canon Boundaries

## User agency test

Remove or rewrite any line that assigns `{{user}}`:

- a voluntary action or position reached through an unstated action;
- dialogue or a promised answer;
- thoughts, feelings, interpretations, attraction, consent, fear, trust, hostility, or desire;
- a relationship, reputation, history, occupation, ability, body detail, or motive not supplied;
- a successful discovery, failure, destiny, special importance, or next decision.

The world may impose involuntary sensory facts or external consequences when the premise supports them, but must still leave interpretation and response open.

Prefer openings like: an NPC asks a question, two demands arrive at once, a practical problem becomes visible, or an anomaly occurs within observable range. End on the unresolved situation—not “you enter,” “you agree,” “you feel,” or “you decide.”

## Canon layers

- **Canon:** user-supplied or explicitly approved facts.
- **Provisional:** generated names, characterization, locations, secrets, pressures, supernatural explanations, scene dressing, and branch ideas.
- **Unresolved:** decisions the seed should keep open rather than fill.

Provisional material is playable draft content, but it must not masquerade as previously approved project canon. When the user approves it, record promotion through Project Steward for linked projects.

## Known settings

Use the user's supplied/approved sources and specified date. Separate:

- verified setting canon;
- reasonable provisional connective material;
- deliberate alternate-universe changes;
- unknown or conflicting claims.

Do not grant setting-specific knowledge, rank, species, magic, technology, or abilities to `{{user}}` without approval. Temporal facts—who is alive, present, affiliated, informed, injured, or capable—must match the chosen timeframe.

## NPC knowledge

For every secret, record:

- who knows;
- who suspects;
- who believes something false;
- who has no access;
- how knowledge can change.

An NPC reacts only to information that reached them through observation, communication, inference, or an approved special capability.
