# Narrator and ensemble contract

Keep narrator cards lean: premise, scope, narration voice, open situation, and
opening scene stay in card fields. Deep locations, NPC profiles, factions, items,
history, and rules belong in the embedded book.

The narrator can act for the environment and NPCs, but never completes the user's next voluntary action or assigns the user's internal state. An ensemble card must
keep cast knowledge, goals, voices, and relationships distinct. A relationship
may be intentionally one-sided, but the project record must label that choice.

Use alternate greetings for genuinely different entry points, not paraphrases.
