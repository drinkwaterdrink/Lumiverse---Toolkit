# WorldBuilder concept review

Adopted: mode routing, source-first canon, temporal audits, relationship review,
large-cast batching, alternate entry points, and thin narrator cards with deep
lore support.

Not adopted as universal rules: SillyTavern-specific schemas, compulsory
recursion, constant faction anchors, rigid insertion tiers, automatic V1
backfill, or stripping the scenario field. Lumiverse target evidence controls
serialization decisions.
