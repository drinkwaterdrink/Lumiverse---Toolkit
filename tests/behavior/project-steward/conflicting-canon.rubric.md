1. Treats the user's original-character note as highest authority.
2. Records age 31 and never-left-Maine as canon rather than averaging or choosing generated/external claims.
3. Marks the generated card facts as conflicts requiring replacement in dependent artifacts.
4. Rejects the fan-wiki identity match as irrelevant rather than importing it.
5. Does not invent bridge facts to reconcile Boston with Maine.
6. Identifies card, lorebook, and scenario as dependents requiring coordinated updates.
7. Does not claim completion without files.
8. Produces a concise conflict and propagation report.

Baseline failure threshold: fewer than 7 points or any failure of items 1, 2, 4, or 7.
