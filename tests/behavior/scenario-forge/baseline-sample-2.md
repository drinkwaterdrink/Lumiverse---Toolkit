# RICH SANDBOX — The Long Weekend

All names and details marked **[PROVISIONAL]** are creative scaffolding, not approved canon.

## CORE

- **Premise:** After a four-hour drive, `{{user}}` reaches an old friend's crowded family home just as the friend leaves for a genuine family emergency. The friend cannot return during the three-day weekend. The household still offers `{{user}}` the promised room, but nobody had prepared to host a near-stranger without their usual social bridge.
- **Play engine:** Awkward hospitality, overlapping household routines, small favors, competing interpretations, and discoveries that can be pursued or ignored.
- **Tone:** Grounded domestic slice of life; layered, sometimes uncomfortable humor; slight supernatural unease that initially resembles faulty memory, old-house behavior, or family eccentricity.
- **Guardrails:** No preset relationship, attraction, hostility, fear, trust, destiny, power, obligation, or emotional response for `{{user}}`. Evidence can invite conclusions but never dictate them.
- **[PROVISIONAL] Setting texture:** A heat-heavy late-summer weekend in a patched-together house where additions, converted rooms, and old plumbing make privacy scarce.

## USER

- `{{user}}` is an adult who drove four hours to spend the weekend with an old friend.
- The visit was expected; the friend's abrupt absence was not.
- `{{user}}` decides whether to stay, leave, help, investigate, socialize, set boundaries, or simply use the house as a place to sleep.
- The household may ask things of `{{user}}`, but requests are not commands and refusals remain viable.
- No narration supplies `{{user}}`'s thoughts, feelings, dialogue, interpretations, or next voluntary action.

## CONFLICT

### Competing pressures

- **Hospitality vs. privacy:** The family wants to honor the invitation without surrendering already scarce space.
- **Repair vs. concealment:** **[PROVISIONAL]** The failing refrigerator demands money and cooperation while Maribel hides the household's financial strain.
- **Departure vs. continuity:** **[PROVISIONAL]** Valeria's possible move threatens routines others quietly rely on.
- **Confession vs. leverage:** **[PROVISIONAL]** Daniela's damaged-car secret gives Eli a reason to hover, but his stranger claim may be genuine.
- **Archive vs. disposal:** **[PROVISIONAL]** Sheryl needs time with the inherited papers before someone clears them out.
- **Explanation vs. escalation:** Misplaced objects, odd sounds, and the impossible room can remain mundane until attention, timing, and contradictory witnesses make that harder.

### Clocks

1. **Emergency absence — fixed:** The friend cannot return during the three-day weekend.
2. **Food spoilage — [PROVISIONAL]:** The refrigerator worsens with each hot day unless repaired, replaced, or unloaded.
3. **Valeria's call — [PROVISIONAL]:** Her private decision arrives before the weekend ends.
4. **Garage truth — [PROVISIONAL]:** Rain forecast for the second night may erase or expose physical evidence.
5. **House inventory — [PROVISIONAL]:** Sheryl expects the papers to be collected or discarded Sunday morning.

### Compact pressure graph

`Crowded hospitality` → strains → `privacy + routines`

`Refrigerator failure` → forces cooperation → exposes `money pressure`

`Daniela's collision` ↔ gives leverage to ↔ `Eli's visits`

`Eli's second sighting` → points toward → `garage / impossible-room clue`

`Sheryl's inventory` → challenges → `family memory` → intensifies → `house unease`

`Valeria's possible move` → destabilizes all practical alliances

Any node may connect to another through a favor, overheard dispute, missing object, or deliberate question; none requires `{{user}}` to investigate.

### Conditional consequences

- Helping with a practical problem can create access or goodwill, never automatic trust.
- Declining involvement preserves boundaries but allows clocks and NPC choices to advance independently.
- Publicly confronting a secret changes who cooperates; private confrontation may create negotiation instead.
- Treating strange evidence as mundane encourages practical tests; treating it as supernatural changes NPC behavior, not objective truth by fiat.
- Leaving the house remains possible; offsite errands, calls, and returns can alter the pressure graph rather than end play.
