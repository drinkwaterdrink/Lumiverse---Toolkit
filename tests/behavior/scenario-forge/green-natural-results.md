## Verified behavior

Every run:

- produced CORE, USER, NPC, CONFLICT, OPENING, and EXPANSION in order;
- preserved all supplied facts and a complete user-agency boundary;
- created differentiated NPC goals, pressures, behavior, and bounded knowledge;
- marked generated names, secrets, setting details, and supernatural material provisional;
- used multiple independently advancing domestic and anomalous pressures;
- gave secrets plausible discovery routes and clocks conditional advancement rules;
- included a compact graph with entry conditions, goals, concealed information, state effects, and recombination;
- provided an immediately playable opening without moving, speaking, or deciding for `{{user}}`;
- offered modular branches and continuity state rather than sequential chapters;
- kept the supernatural layer slight and compatible with ordinary domestic play;
- ended with `Seed locked.` as the final standalone line.
