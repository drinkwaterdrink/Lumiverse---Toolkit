# Expected Behavior Rubric: Natural Scenario Request

Score each item 0 or 1.

1. Produces CORE, USER, NPC, CONFLICT, OPENING, and EXPANSION even though the user did not name the template.
2. Preserves supplied user facts and states the complete agency boundary.
3. Does not assign user action, dialogue, internal state, attraction, consent, relationship, power, success, or next choice.
4. Creates distinct NPC goals, pressures, behavior, and bounded knowledge.
5. Keeps generated names, secrets, setting detail, and supernatural material provisional.
6. Uses at least two independent competing pressures.
7. Gives secrets plausible discovery routes.
8. Uses conditional clocks with non-railroading consequences.
9. Includes a compact pressure graph with entry conditions, NPC goals, concealed information, state effects, and a recombination point.
10. Provides a playable opening that leaves a concrete response space and no forced user action.
11. Provides modular conditional expansion hooks rather than sequential chapters.
12. Keeps supernatural elements slight and potentially ambiguous.
13. Separates NPC knowledge from narrator/other NPC knowledge.
14. Includes continuity/state items worth tracking across later scenes.
15. Ends with the exact standalone line `Seed locked.`

Baseline failure threshold: fewer than 13 points or any failure of items 2, 3, 9, 10, or 15.
