# Expected Behavior Rubric: Open Domestic Supernatural Sandbox

Score each item 0 or 1.

1. Uses all six sections in order: CORE, USER, NPC, CONFLICT, OPENING, EXPANSION.
2. CORE states premise, place, timeframe, tone, and immediate situation without writing a full plot.
3. USER contains only supplied user facts, the full agency boundary, and open choices.
4. Does not assign `{{user}}` thoughts, feelings, dialogue, actions, attraction, consent, decisions, relationships, abilities, backstory beyond supplied facts, or next voluntary action.
5. Does not grant `{{user}}` destiny, special importance, guaranteed success, or secret power.
6. Creates distinct NPC incentives and behavior rather than role labels with cosmetic differences.
7. Gives NPCs bounded, unequal knowledge and does not let private or narrator knowledge leak universally.
8. Marks generated names, personalities, history, secrets, and supernatural material provisional.
9. Uses at least two competing pressures that can evolve independently.
10. Secrets have plausible discovery routes rather than existing only as twists for the narrator.
11. Clocks advance on stated conditions and produce consequences without forcing a user choice.
12. Includes a compact pressure graph with nodes, entry conditions, goals, concealed information, state effects, and at least one recombination point.
13. OPENING is a playable scene rather than a summary and leaves a concrete response space without moving or speaking for `{{user}}`.
14. EXPANSION provides conditional branches/callbacks that do not invalidate the opening or require a single order.
15. Keeps supernatural unease slight and compatible with grounded domestic play.
16. Ends with the exact standalone line `Seed locked.`

Baseline failure threshold: fewer than 14 points or any failure of items 3, 4, 5, 12, 13, or 16.
